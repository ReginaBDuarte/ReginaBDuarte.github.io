About

Research that takes human judgment seriously.

I study what happens when humans and AI make decisions together: how behavior changes when AI enters the loop, and how to design systems that genuinely improve human judgment rather than just replace it.

I'm a PhD researcher at Instituto Superior Técnico and the Center for Responsible AI. My work spans clinical decision-making, fake news detection, and group dynamics, with publications at ACM CHI and ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare.

Before the PhD, I spent three years as a data scientist in banking and consulting, working on credit risk models, regulatory compliance, and retail forecasting.

Beyond research, I care about how science reaches decision-makers in government, institutions, and public policy broadly. I think AI has an important role there too: not just as a policy subject, but as a tool to make the path from evidence to action faster and more effective.

If you work on responsible AI, behavioral design, HCI, or evidence-based policy, I'd love to connect.

— The problem

More accurate AI doesn't mean better decisions.

People over-rely on confident systems and under-rely on ones they distrust. The goal isn't more trust — it's calibrated reliance.

↑
Over-reliance

Under-reliance

— The idea

Explanations work — but only when they help you say no.

A good explanation isn't decoration. It should make it easier to override the AI when it's wrong — not just nod along when it's right.

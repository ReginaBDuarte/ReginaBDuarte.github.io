**Card** — rounded surface container. `default` is white with a hairline; `iris`/`accent` are tinted; `soft`/`sunken` are flat fills for grouping; `float` adds a lifted shadow.

```jsx
<Card padding={2} interactive>…</Card>
<Card variant="iris" padding={3}>…</Card>
```

Padding steps: 0/1/2/3 → 0/16/24/32px.
